# QA & Playtest audit, round 1: stability and bugs

Audit only. No code or committed files were changed except this report.
Date: 2026-11-02. Branch `claude/uic-campus-game-plan-btnm3c` at 9c65098.

## Summary

Stability is in good shape: no P0. The one P1 (QA-11) is about bot behaviour, not crashes. Five full `?squad=bots` matches (one on normal zone timing, four with `zoneFast`, including three Play-agains in a row) ended correctly and produced **zero page errors and zero console errors**. Nothing leaked across Play again: scene children, Rapier bodies and colliders stayed at 282 / 16 / 2593 and the JS heap stayed at 539-543 MB over four rounds. Nobody fell through the world (121-point ground grid: 0 falls, 0 slides, 0 jitter; 25 roof boxes walkable). Daley Library, University Hall and 49 of 52 Atlas glb doors can be walked into; every staircase can be climbed. The die / downed / spectate / end-screen flow works.

What I found is mostly small, cheap bugs (P2): a DOM leak on every Play again (QA-04), a wrong "Survived" time on the first match (QA-01), three SEL/SEO doors blocked by a wall collider (QA-05), cheat hooks in the public build (QA-02), nine loot crates inside geometry (QA-06), and two tests that time out under CPU load (QA-09). The one larger item is QA-11: enemy bots stand still until the zone reaches them and then walk slower than the zone moves.

Limits of this audit, so nobody over-trusts it:
- Software WebGL on a shared 4-CPU machine. FPS, boot times and the "gfx.ready timed out" fallback are not representative of a student laptop and I report no performance numbers.
- To run whole matches I **stubbed `renderer.render`** and **time-warped `performance.now` x4**. That proves game logic, restarts, leaks and DOM state, not visuals. I took no usable screenshots, so no visual finding is claimed.
- My walking player was a naive "head for the zone centre" walker, which walks into the Daley Library facade next to the spawn (the player stopped at (20.1, 31.5), beside the door at z 35.1). So I have no evidence either way about bots or players getting stuck in long walks. (Separately, QA-11 records that enemy bots stand still; that is a behaviour finding, not a stuck-in-geometry one.)
- Not tested: upper floors of University Hall, furniture collision inside buildings, shooting through walls with a real rifle (I rely on the Rapier-ray design; the old playtest tool is too noisy to prove it, see QA-08), healing/reload spam timing, third-person camera in tight rooms.
- Probe scripts are in the session scratchpad, not committed: `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-UIC-campus-game/b2425d9f-3460-5955-b585-ba69fe169a3a/scratchpad/` (`fast.mjs`, `explore.mjs`, `probe2.mjs`). QA-08 proposes turning them into the real tool. Raw results are in `screenshots/tmp/audit/qa/*.json` (gitignored).

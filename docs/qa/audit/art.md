# Art / Looks audit (Art Director, Round 1)

Status: COMPLETE (Round 1). 14 findings: 0 P0, 8 P1, 6 P2.
Scope: lighting, sky, exposure, shadows, materials, code-built vs Atlas .glb consistency, characters, rifle, effects, trees, ground, interiors, far LOD, flicker, z-fighting, seams.
Reference: PUBG tone (muted natural palette, readable silhouettes, atmospheric depth), real UIC, student laptop.
Images are in `screenshots/tmp/audit/art/` (gitignored). Rendered at 1600x900 with software WebGL (SwiftShader), `?quality=medium` unless stated.
Auto quality on this machine resolves to **low** (SwiftShader matches `WEAK_RENDERER` in `client/src/render/quality.ts`, which turns off post, AO, bloom, SMAA and uses the procedural sky).

Severity: P0 broken, P1 major vs the owner's bar, P2 polish.

## Not found / checked and fine

- Flicker or z-fighting: none seen in the 12 frames rendered. Not a full search.
- Floating or sinking props: none seen in the frames rendered.
- Ground textures (sidewalk, grass, asphalt) are at a believable scale at eye level.
- Medium vs high: `high/combat-range.png` looks the same as medium at 1600x900. Higher shadow cascades were not visible in these frames.
- Interior lighting (library reading room, UH lobby) is well exposed and consistent with outdoors.

## Method and limits

- 12 frames rendered: map-quad-ground (medium, high, auto, bridges), uh-from-quad (medium, high), map-sce (medium, bridges), combat-range (medium, high), uh-lobby, library-reading-room, b4-uh-far, plus a character set from `tools/screenshot/character-shots.mjs` (copy with ports changed, in `screenshots/tmp/audit/art/char-shots.mjs`).
- Character animation: the player test course shows no rifle on the local player (unarmed until looting), so the animation-pose check on the player body only covers locomotion. Rifle poses were judged on the practice dummies.
- Software WebGL only. Tone mapping, SMAA and AO behave the same, but frame rate and shadow filtering may differ on a real GPU.
- ART-03 is from measured UVs in the glb files, with `map-sce.png` showing the large board marks. A side-by-side with a code-built wall would confirm it.

# AI audit (round 1): bots vs the owner's bar

Auditor: AI Engineer. Branch `claude/uic-campus-game-plan-btnm3c` at 9c65098. Audit only, no code changed.
Bar (docs/TEAM.md, "Bots"): plausible human players that use cover, rotate with the zone, loot, heal and reposition; good-but-human aim (reaction time, spread, misses at range); never stuck more than 10 s; never shoot through walls; can be outplayed and can win.

Status: DRAFT, written incrementally. Findings marked "code" are confirmed by reading the source (file:line). Findings marked "measured" come from the headless harness (see Method). The measured-numbers table is filled in at the end.

## Method

- Code read: all of `client/src/squad/**` plus the files the adapter calls into (`player/botGun.ts`, `botSight.ts`, `wallProbe.ts`, `botBodies.ts`, `zone/zoneLogic.ts`, `combat/balance.ts`).
- Headless runs: a private Playwright script (kept in the scratchpad, not committed) loads `?squad=bots&zoneFast=1` from a Vite server on port 5305, patches the served `main.ts` and `adapter.ts` in memory (no repo file touched) to expose bot internals, then steps the simulation at 30 Hz without rendering. A simple autopilot walks the human player toward the safe zone. It records per-bot stuck time, intents, shots, who each shot hit, and whether a visible mesh sits between the muzzle and a hit point.

## Findings (code-verified, in order found)

Severity: P0 breaks the bar badly or blocks a match, P1 clearly misses the bar, P2 polish / realism.

| AI-01 | P0 | Enemy squad leaders never move except for the zone, so enemy squads are stationary campers | `adapter.ts:739-741`: for an enemy bot `leaderId` is the first living squad member, which for that member itself is `body.id`. `botIntent` then returns `follow` with target = self, and `stepToward` with stopAt 6 never moves (`bots.ts:70`, `adapter.ts:824-831`). The other three members follow that leader. In the smoke run squads C and D spent 20.0 of 20.0 s in `follow` with 0.0 m travelled; squad B moved only because the zone sent it. | Give enemy squads their own goals: loot route, then a rotation point inside the next circle, then hunt toward the nearest known squad or sound. Leader needs a `moveTo` target, not `follow` itself. | M | AI Engineer |
| AI-02 | P1 | Bots react instantly: no reaction time | `botAim.ts:24-28` `botTrigger`: cooldown starts at 0, so the first shot leaves on the same frame the foe is first seen (`adapter.ts:857-859`). The only delay is the 0.5 s interval after that. Bar asks for human reaction time. | Add a per-target acquisition delay (for example 0.25 to 0.6 s, randomised, longer for a foe who appears at the edge of view or beyond 20 m) before the first shot. | S | AI Engineer |
| AI-03 | P1 | Bots see 360 degrees through their own back, and notice foes at any angle | `adapter.ts:712-726`: foes within 28 m with a clear ray are "seen" with no field-of-view or facing test; the body is then snapped to face them (`adapter.ts:854`, no turn rate). A bot is never surprised from behind. | Field of view of about 110 degrees plus a short "heard shots / took damage" override; limit turn speed (for example 300 degrees per second) so flanking works. | M | AI Engineer |
| AI-04 | P1 | Bots never use cover and never crouch, they walk straight at the target | `adapter.ts:843-853`: the `shoot` action is `stepToward(foe, stopAt 4)` at 0.85 x walk speed: it closes to 4 m and stands in the open. The only crouch flag is for downed bots (`adapter.ts:647`, `poseBot`). Nothing in `squad/**` searches for cover, strafes or peeks. | Add a cover picker (sample points around the bot, keep those a ray from the foe blocks but a short step re-exposes), peek-shoot-duck cycle, and crouch while shooting from cover. Retreat to cover to reload and heal. | L | AI Engineer |
| AI-05 | P1 | Bots only ever walk (1.47 m/s) and the zone edge moves faster than that | `adapter.ts:635` `botSpeed = walkSpeed x 1.05 = 1.47 m/s`; player run speed is 4.5 m/s (`player/config.ts:12`). The first circle is about 931 m wide in radius and shrinks to 0.7x in 60 s, so the edge moves about 4.6 m/s (`zoneLogic.ts:55-62`). A bot starting 150 to 400 m out cannot stay ahead of it; later phases do 12 dps. | Let bots sprint when outside or about to be outside the next circle and nobody is near (cost: stamina or noise if you add it). Start rotation earlier than `rotateLeadS: 25` (`botZone.ts:21`) for distant bots. | S | AI Engineer |
| AI-06 | P1 | Bots never use armor, medkits or grenades, and bandage only if they happen to find one | `botLoot.ts:208` `BOT_LOOT_KINDS = ['gun','ammo','bandage']`; `startBandages: 0` (`botLoot.ts:200`); heal only when calm and below 75 HP (`botLoot.ts:280`). Armor and med kits on the floor are ignored. A bot hurt in a fight stays hurt. | Add armor and med kit to the wanted kinds, use them when calm; consider starting bandages 1 to 2 so healing is visible. | S | AI Engineer |
| AI-07 | P2 | A bot with no ammo still charges to 4 m and stands there | `adapter.ts:843-860`: `shoot` intent does not check `botCanShoot` / `botDry` for movement, only for the trigger. A dry bot walks up to its foe and stands doing nothing. | If dry: switch to `moveTo` cover / retreat / loot ammo, or follow leader. | S | AI Engineer |
| AI-08 | P2 | Bots "see" foes behind a teammate and fire into the teammate | `botSight.ts` ignores people; `botGun.fire` stops at the first collider that is not the shooter (`botGun.ts:42-52`); `shootBot` then returns `blocked` for a same-squad hit (`friendlyFire.ts`). | AI-09 | P2 | Engagement range is 28 m but the weapon range constant is 40 m, so bots never fight beyond 28 m | `adapter.ts:719` (`d < 28`) versus `botAim.ts:BOT_AIM.range = 40`. Aim spread table (`spreadAt10` 0.45) is therefore never exercised past 28 m. "Misses at range" in the bar is only partly met. | Raise detection to match range (with FOV, see AI-03) or lower the constant, and make long shots miss more often. | S | AI Engineer |
